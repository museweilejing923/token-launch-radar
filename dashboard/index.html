The formula

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

Every number comes from the sealed snapshot. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row.

Category multiples

The winsorised median mcap / TVL of the live-token protocols in each category (at least 3 peers). Categories without enough live peers use the global median.

What “pre-token” means here

A protocol is pre-token when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. That is what the snapshot records — it cannot prove the protocol has no token, and this board never claims it does. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left out of the ranking, as are exchanges and versioned pools.

The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: the 40 protocols at or above $50M TVL that meet the house rule are all on the board, and the board goes on to rank every other rankable pre-token protocol below the floor — the early ones this radar exists to find.
